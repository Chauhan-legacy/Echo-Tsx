# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-24)

### Core Features
- Standalone zero-dependency TypeScript forensic DSP and neural network library.
- Arbitrary-length FFT with Radix-2 Cooley-Tukey and Bluestein Chirp-Z.
- Thomson DPSS multitaper spectral estimation and Daubechies D4 Wavelet Packet Decomposition.
- Dense linear algebra core: Householder QR, Cholesky, Cyclic Jacobi Eigen-decomposition, and Jacobi SVD.
- Forensic signal modules: Schroeder RT60, CUSUM/BOCPD change-point detection, PRNU photo-response non-uniformity with PCE, rPPG pulse extraction (CHROM/POS), YIN pitch tracking, MUSIC/ESPRIT direction of arrival, FastICA, and robust statistics.
- Client-side deep learning: Dilated depthwise separable 1D convolutions, multi-head attention with ALiBi, AdamW optimizer, ECE calibration, and integrated gradients explainability.
