# Third-Party Notices & Scientific Citations (echotrace-math)

No third-party runtime code is bundled into `echotrace-math`.
`echotrace-math` has **zero runtime dependencies** (`"dependencies": {}`).

All mathematical algorithms were authored from first principles in pure TypeScript according to published scientific literature:

1. **Bluestein's Chirp-Z Transform:** L. I. Bluestein, *IEEE Trans. Audio Electroacoustics*, 1970.
2. **ENF Grid Forensics:** C. Grigoras, *Int. J. Speech Lang. Law*, 2005.
3. **PRNU Camera Identification:** J. Lukas, J. Fridrich, M. Goljan, *IEEE TIFS*, 2006.
4. **Remote PPG (CHROM & POS):** G. de Haan & V. Jeanne (2013); W. Wang et al. (2017), *IEEE TBME*.
5. **Schroeder Reverberation Decay:** M. R. Schroeder (1965); A. Lundeby et al. (1995), *Applied Acoustics*.
6. **ALiBi Attention:** O. Press, N. A. Smith, M. Lewis, *ICLR*, 2022.
7. **MUSIC & ESPRIT Subspace Estimation:** R. Schmidt (1986); R. Roy & T. Kailath (1989), *IEEE Transactions on ASSP*.
8. **Kahan Compensated Summation:** W. Kahan, *CACM*, 1965.
